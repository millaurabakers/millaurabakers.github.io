Our Story

From the Heart of Anand

"Millaura" was born from two words we hold dear — millets, the grain our grandmothers swore by, and aura, the warmth a home kitchen leaves on everyone who walks through the door.

We're baking out of Anand, Gujarat — a town better known for its milk than its millets, but one that taught us the same lesson either way: real nourishment is never rushed. Long before "wellness" was a trend, our families were grinding bajra and jowar by hand, turning humble grains into something that felt like a hug on a plate.

Millaura Bakers is our attempt to carry that feeling forward — recipes tested in a home kitchen, shared with neighbours before anyone else, refined one batch at a time until they tasted like the ones we grew up on. We're not open yet. We're still in that kitchen, getting it right, because Anand raised us to believe a cookie is worth doing properly.

— With love, from our kitchen to yours
